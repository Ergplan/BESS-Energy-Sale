    Transmission & exchange

    No CTU charges on selling. Source: SRPC RTA, billing period May 2026 (billing month July 2026). Karnataka drawee DICs (5,952 MW GNA) paid ₹235.8 Cr, which is ₹3.96 lakh/MW/month. T-GNA rate under Reg. 11 of the CERC Sharing Regulations 2020, as amended = charges × 1.10 ÷ (days × 96 × GNA) = ₹146.4/MW/block = ₹586/MWh. The MoP ISTS waiver of June 2025 covers only BESS co-located with RE, so it does not apply to IEX-purchase charging.

    Financing & WACC

    Equal principal repayments from year 1, interest on the opening balance. DSCR excludes augmentation (assumed funded from a reserve) and the equity-funded cost. WACC = Ke × E + Kd × (1 − tax) × D. The model discounts NPV and levelised cost at WACC.

    Finance & O&M

    Method
    • Sizing: energy to sell = night connectivity × window hours. The BESS nameplate is that energy grossed up for discharge loss, HVAC load, trafo/line loss, depth of discharge and year-1 average fade. The BESS power rating equals the connectivity.
    • Own-solar charging: solar AC capacity = sizing % × daily charging need ÷ (CUF × 24 h), capped at the connectivity. DC capacity and land follow each plant's DC/AC ratio and acres per MWp. Charging costs nothing per MWh because the solar is built for the BESS and its capex is in the project. When the battery is full, surplus solar is sold in the day (or curtailed, if you choose), using the highest-priced blocks. Solar produced during the discharge window is curtailed. No CTU drawal charges apply.
    • IEX-purchase charging: standalone BESS drawing at up to the connectivity MW. The charge window is lengthened to cover ISTS and line losses. Purchases pay the IEX fee plus STOA (T-GNA, ₹/MWh) or LTA (GNA, fixed ₹/MW/month); sales pay no CTU charge.
    • Prices: IEX MCP in 15-minute blocks over 365 days (), one cycle a day. A window that crosses midnight uses that day's early-morning blocks.
    • Returns: unlevered project IRR, pre- and post-tax. Depreciation is straight-line over the project life, with no loss carry-forward. Fade is linear and augmentation restores capacity. Equity IRR uses the debt share, interest rate and tenor in Financing; interest is tax-deductible. NPV and levelised cost are discounted at WACC. Levelised cost = PV of all costs, net of solar surplus sales ÷ PV of BESS MWh sold. The model assumes the plant's bids do not move the clearing price.
